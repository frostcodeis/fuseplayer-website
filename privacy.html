Privacy Policy

FusePlayer 1.0 (Android version code 4) · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Your library can generate online requests. Titles derived from files and folders can be sent to metadata and subtitle providers. Watch tracking sends playback events when enabled. The controls and recipients are explained below.

1. Who is responsible

FusePlayer is developed by Kamil Michalak, trading as FrostCode, Bjarkarholt 9, 270 Mosfellsbaer, Iceland. Privacy contact: frostcodeis@gmail.com. This policy covers FusePlayer and its product website.

FusePlayer is a media player. It does not provide, sell or grant access to films, television programmes, channels or broadcasts. You provide your own media and sources and must have permission to use them. Purchasing the player does not include video content or external subscriptions.

2. Information on your device

The app accesses the media and locations you select. It stores library and connection settings, paths, playback history and progress, personal ratings, subtitle selections, saved credentials and API keys, SIMKL and Trakt tokens, cached metadata and artwork, and downloaded subtitles.

No FrostCode account is required. The reviewed release does not use a FrostCode-operated application backend. Video files are not uploaded to the metadata or subtitle-search providers described here. Titles and other information about those files can nevertheless be sent.

3. Metadata and images

SIMKL: when your account is connected, requests to api.simkl.com can include the title or search text, content type, title or series identifier, authorization token and application identification. Responses provide descriptions, ratings, dates, genres and episode information. SIMKL artwork is retrieved from simkl.in through wsrv.nl; wsrv.nl receives the image URL and image parameters.

Movie of the Night: requests to api.movieofthenight.com include the title, content type, language, country setting and your own API key, which you enter and save in Settings → Library and Accounts → Movie of the Night. The country is Poland when the app language is Polish; otherwise it comes from the device locale, with Poland used as a fallback. GPS is not used for this feature. Responses provide streaming availability and can supplement descriptions, ratings, genres and artwork. Without a saved Movie of the Night key, FusePlayer does not send requests to this API or use its metadata cache. No shared publisher key is supplied. Requests use your provider account and plan limits; any provider fees are separate from the app purchase. A SIMKL or Trakt account is not required for this integration.

Information and artwork can also come from user-provided files next to the video. Having local artwork does not prevent additional API requests. Metadata requests can occur while displaying the library, folders, details and Continue watching. A cleaned filename is not anonymous. Avoid adding files whose names you do not want disclosed through these searches.

4. SIMKL and Trakt watch tracking

Account connection uses the provider's device authorization flow. You approve access with the provider; FusePlayer does not ask for your SIMKL or Trakt password in its own connection form.

Completing account connection currently enables watch-history sending. When enabled, playback events sent to the connected service include the title identifier, season and episode where relevant, playback progress, and start, pause or stop events. Matching an item can send its title and year. You can turn history sending off in settings. Your personal FusePlayer ratings are stored locally and are not sent by the reviewed rating feature.

Disabling history does not disable metadata requests or erase events already received. Removing local tokens does not delete the external account, its history or necessarily revoke authorization. Manage these with the provider. Profile visibility is controlled by the provider's account settings.

5. Subtitles

Subtitle features are optional, but opening subtitle search starts a search automatically. Changing a provider or language can cause further requests. Queries include the title or entered text, language, and season and episode where applicable.

  • SubDL: requests to api.subdl.com use your own API key, included in the request URL. FusePlayer does not require your SubDL password. Downloads can use dl.subdl.com or an address returned by the provider.
  • OpenSubtitles.com: api.opensubtitles.com receives an API key and, when account details are used, your username and password for authentication, followed by a token. Download requests identify the subtitle.

Downloading contacts the host supplied in the result. It receives the requested URL, headers and connection IP address. Files can be stored and reused locally. Provider accounts and quotas are managed by their respective services.

6. Network sources and connection security

Your selected servers receive the credentials and directory, path and media-read requests needed for FTP, SMB, SFTP or HTTP/HTTPS playback. Saved credentials can be reused for later scans. Android document providers, including cloud providers you choose, may process listing and read requests.

FTP and HTTP do not encrypt traffic. HTTPS uses TLS; SFTP uses SSH with server-key checking. The app does not guarantee SMB session encryption. Dynamic subtitle links are not restricted to a single host or necessarily to HTTPS. Network recipients can see your connection IP address. Not all app traffic is encrypted.

7. Diagnostics

The reviewed app has no advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or Sentry. Android playback diagnostics may process technical playback events, formats, errors, timings, counters and session identifiers. Android, Google or the manufacturer may further process diagnostics depending on device settings. There is no separate FusePlayer switch for all system diagnostics. System font providers may also perform font-related requests.

8. Security, backups and retention

Saved credentials, user API keys and account tokens are protected using AES-256-GCM and Android Keystore after any required migration. Hardware-backed storage depends on the device. This does not encrypt every file or network connection.

Library, history and settings remain for ongoing use until removed using app or Android controls. Metadata is cached to reduce repeat requests; disk metadata entries are generally configured for refresh after 30 days. Removing an account does not remove every cached item or downloaded subtitle. Saving or removing the Movie of the Night key clears metadata caches and refreshes library details; a request already in progress may finish. Removing a key does not delete the provider account or its records.

Android backup is enabled, with rules excluding the app's DataStore and saved SFTP server-key pins. Downloaded subtitles and filenames are not excluded by those rules. Manual exports can contain library details, paths, preferences and, if selected, a passphrase-protected credentials section. Keep backup files secure. Deleting local data does not delete exported copies, historical system backups or provider records.

Requests already started can continue after you leave a screen or hide the app. External providers apply their own retention rules; we do not promise that their processing is only ephemeral.

9. Your choices and requests

You can choose sources, remove the Movie of the Night key to stop new requests to that API, avoid opening subtitle search, remove saved credentials and connections, disconnect accounts, disable watch-history sending and use Android cache/storage controls. There is no single app switch stopping all automatic metadata requests while retaining every library feature. Account deletion and authorization revocation must also be managed directly with providers. Clearing app storage does not remove videos stored elsewhere.

Consult SIMKL, Movie of the Night, wsrv.nl, Trakt, SubDL and OpenSubtitles.com. External services may process information outside Iceland or the EEA. We do not control their logs or promise a single retention period or processing country.
