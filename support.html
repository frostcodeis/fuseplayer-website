Common questions

Which devices is FusePlayer designed for?

FusePlayer focuses on Android TV and Google TV and also supports Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or later. Playback support depends on the device and media format.

Does FusePlayer provide films?

FusePlayer is a media player. It does not provide or sell films, TV shows or channels. You provide your own media and must have permission to use it. Buying the player does not include video content or external subscriptions.

My FTP or SMB source does not connect. What should I check?

Check that your device can reach the server, that the server is running, and that the address, port, share name and credentials are correct. Check access permissions on the server. Ordinary FTP does not encrypt network traffic; use it only where you understand and accept that risk.

Where can I ask a privacy question?

Email frostcodeis@gmail.com. Our Privacy Policy explains metadata requests, external integrations, system diagnostics, saved credentials and data controls.

Who is FusePlayer intended for?

FusePlayer is intended for users aged 13 and over. External services may have higher age requirements in your country.

How do optional accounts and subtitles work?

SIMKL and Trakt are connected through their own device-code or QR authorization pages. Movie of the Night is optional: save your own API key in Settings → Library and Accounts → Movie of the Night to enable streaming availability and supplementary metadata and images. With no saved key, that API is not queried. SubDL uses your own API key; OpenSubtitles.com requires your own API key in subtitle settings and can also use your own account credentials. FusePlayer has no shared OpenSubtitles key. Without your key, this provider is not queried. External terms, download quotas and subscriptions apply separately and are not included in the price of FusePlayer. When reporting subtitle problems, tell us which provider you selected, your app version and the error message, without sending keys or passwords.

How can I remove my saved information?

Use the app's controls to remove saved connections and account credentials, disconnect services or turn watch-history sending off. Android's app-storage controls can remove local app data. These actions do not delete your external provider accounts, previously sent history, exported backups or source videos. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where are purchase help and library licenses?

Purchases and refunds are handled through Google Play. See the Google Play refund information, our Terms of Use and the Licenses and Sources page.
